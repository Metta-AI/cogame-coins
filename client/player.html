COINS — seat

This seat is view-only in the browser. A Coins policy is a prompt: the player container's only job is to deliver it, and every decision is made in the game server, which sends BOTH seats' prompts plus their observations to Claude as one parallel batch per beat.

Seat channel

The seat itself is the websocket /player?slot=N&token=T speaking coins.player.v1: one prompt frame on connect (and again after welcome, which guards the slot-registration race), then welcome, one state frame per beat close, and final with the scores. A bad slot or token is a 403 and a second connection on a live slot is a 409.

{"type": "prompt", "prompt": "<your strategy, <= 4000 chars>",
 "scripted": "", "policy": "coins-truce"}

scripted may be honest, greedy, reciprocator or tit-for-tat to register the seat as a built-in baseline instead; the server plays those deterministically with no LLM at all.

Replying

The game asks the model for exactly one JSON object per beat:

{"intent": "take_mine", "say": "your coins are yours",
 "notes": "Cobalt stole 2 in beat 5; watching now"}

intent is one of take_mine, take_any, take_theirs, guard, hold. say is capped at 48 characters and is spectator-only — the other cog never reads it. notes is capped at 300 characters and comes back to you, and only you, next beat. Both caps are counted in runes, not bytes.

Watching

The room renders at /client/global off the same page the hosted static replay bundle serves; live spectator frames are the /global websocket.
